Theatre

I’ve been active in a local theater group for several years now. For as long as I can remember, I’ve loved acting. On stage, I can step away from the real world for a moment and become someone completely different. Playing a character, behaving in a way that doesn’t suit me at all, and always trying something new—that’s exactly what I love about theater.

Over the past few years, I’ve had the chance to play a wide variety of roles. From a grumpy campground owner to a camel: no role is too outlandish for me. For me, theater is a wonderful way to be creative, challenge myself, and, above all, have a lot of fun. I hope to be able to keep doing this for many years to come.

Music

Music has played a major role in my life for over 14 years. I play the flugelhorn in a music club, a hobby that lets me channel a lot of energy and have a lot of fun. For me, making music is more than just playing on my own: it’s the rehearsing together, performing, and working toward a big project that makes it so much fun.

Over the past few years, I've had the opportunity to work on many wonderful projects. From a concert with the "Zandtovenaar" to a major concert celebrating the 700th anniversary of my home town Nieuw-Lekkerland.

What I love about music is that you can create something together that you could never create on your own. And the bigger and more challenging the project, the more I enjoy it.

Videography

In addition to what happens on stage, I also find it interesting to work behind the scenes on technical aspects. At church, I help organize the livestreams together with others, and I’m in charge of the various aspects of video technology. Among other things, I work with Blackmagic ATEM and various cameras.

At the Grafisch Lyceum Rotterdam, I got hooked on this hobby during my studies, and ever since, I’ve loved doing it. From operating and switching between shots during a livestream to connecting and configuring equipment: I enjoy understanding how it all works together.

Efteling Enthusiast

To me, Efteling is more than just an amusement park. I’m a huge fan of everything that’s conceived and built there: from attractions and roller coasters to stories, sets, and technology.

Thanks to my membership, I visit the park regularly. Every time, there’s always something new to discover. Whether it’s a detail in an attraction that I’d never noticed before, a new show, or simply a day spent enjoying the atmosphere.
